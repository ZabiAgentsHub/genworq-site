Platform

One platform. A crew of agents. Nothing running blind.

Every GENWORQ solution runs on the same platform: one system that assigns work to the right agent, checks it against your rules, and keeps a record of what happened. This is how it’s built.

The GENWORQ platform is the coordination layer that routes incoming work to the right agent, applies your approval rules before anything irreversible happens, and logs every action. The single system every GENWORQ solution runs on top of, not five separate tools.

The Orchestrator

How does the Genworq platform work?

Work arrives the way it already does: a form fill, an inbound call, an invoice landing in the inbox, a record changing in your CRM. The platform watches those entry points across CRM, ERP, Notify, Analytics, Docs and Inbox, and treats each one as a trigger rather than something a person has to notice first.

From there it works out what the task actually is and which agent should handle it. Around twelve agents sit behind the orchestrator, each with its own remit: one drafts and sends campaigns, another qualifies leads, another matches invoices. The platform reads the context, picks the agent whose job it is, and hands the work over with everything that agent needs to act on it.

When something is strategic, sensitive or irreversible, it stops. Those are the approval points you set, and the platform holds the work there rather than guessing. Anything it is not confident about gets held for a person instead of pushed through, and every action it does take is logged and reversible.

Integrations & architecture

Runs inside what you already use

Slack, HubSpot, Microsoft 365, Supabase, n8n, and whatever else you’re already running. The platform builds on top of your existing accounts package, CRM, inbox and spreadsheets rather than replacing them. Nothing gets thrown out, and nobody has to learn a new system.

The tools we build with are open, well-known software you can look inside, not something only we know how to fix. Your information stays in one place, so the numbers finally add up, and something is usually up and running in weeks rather than after a two-year IT project.

Designed for serious operations

Powerful autonomy. Visible control.

GENWORQ is engineered to help teams deploy agentic systems with confidence, not black boxes.

Human checkpoints

Set clear approval points for strategic, sensitive, or irreversible actions. You decide which steps always need sign-off, and the platform holds work at those points rather than pushing it through and asking later.

Decision trails

Understand what agents used, why they decided, and where work moved next. Every action is written down, so you can always check what happened instead of taking it on trust.

Guardrails by design

Define policy, access, and operational constraints before agents take action. The limits are set up front rather than bolted on after something has already gone wrong.

Common questions

What teams ask about the platform.

Is this one platform or several separate tools?

One platform. The five solutions, marketing, sales, voice, chat and back office, all run on top of the same coordination layer, using the same approval rules and the same decision trail. You are not wiring together five disconnected systems.

Does it replace the software we already use?

No. We build on top of your existing accounts package, CRM, inbox and spreadsheets. Nothing gets thrown out and nobody learns a new system.

What happens if an agent gets something wrong?

Every action is logged and reversible. Anything the system is not confident about gets held for a human instead of guessed at, and you decide which steps always need sign-off.

How is our data handled?

It stays in your systems and stays yours. Work runs inside the tools you already use rather than being copied into a separate platform, and every action is logged so you can check exactly what was touched.

Can we start with just one agent, or does it require the whole platform?

Start with one. We begin with a single process rather than your whole operation, so you see it working before you commit further. Most first processes go live in two to four weeks. The rest of the platform is there when you want to scale into it.
