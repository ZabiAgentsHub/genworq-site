How it works

How does sales automation with Genworq work? Three steps.

01STEP 01

Every new lead gets an answer immediately

Form fill, email, ad click or chat, it gets a real reply within minutes, not whenever someone next checks the inbox. Speed to lead is the single biggest thing most teams are losing on, and it costs nothing to fix.

  • Replies in minutes
  • Nights and weekends
  • Every channel
02STEP 02

Only the real ones reach your team

Each lead is qualified in the conversation, what they need, when, and whether the budget is there. The ready ones book themselves into your calendar. The rest stay in nurture instead of clogging the pipeline.

  • Qualified in conversation
  • Books itself
  • Nothing goes cold
03STEP 03

The follow-up nobody has time for

The people who opened and never replied, the quote sent three weeks ago, the meeting that got rescheduled and forgotten, all chased automatically, and it stops the moment they respond.

  • Chases the openers
  • Revives old quotes
  • Stops when they reply

    Common questions

    The things people ask first.

    Does this replace our sales team?

    No. It removes the admin around selling, first response, qualification, chasing, so your team spends its time on the conversations that need a person.

    Will the replies sound automated?

    They’re written in your voice and reference what the lead actually asked. Most prospects can’t tell, and the ones who can mostly don’t mind being answered quickly.

    Does it work with our CRM?

    We build on top of what you use, HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive or a spreadsheet if that’s genuinely where things live.

    What counts as a qualified lead?

    Whatever you say it is. We set the criteria with you before anything goes live.

    How many times will it chase someone before it stops?

    You set the cadence, and it stops the moment someone replies. The default runs about 4 to 6 messages spread over two weeks, shorter than a cold-outreach sequence, since these are people who already showed interest, not cold prospects. Anyone who answers, books, or opts out drops out of it immediately. The aim is to catch the people who meant to reply and forgot, not to wear anyone down.
