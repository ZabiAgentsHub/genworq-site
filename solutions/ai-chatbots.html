SOLUTIONS / AI CHATBOTS

Answer visitors before they leave.

Most people who land on your site have one question standing between them and a decision. A chatbot trained on your actual documents answers it in seconds, then qualifies them or books them in, instead of handing them a contact form.

An AI chatbot is software on your website that answers visitor questions in conversation. Unlike a scripted menu, it draws answers from your real documents, pricing, policies, product detail, and can qualify or book the visitor before they leave.

How it works

Three steps to work that does itself.

01STEP 01

It learns what you actually sell

We feed it your pricing, your policies, your product pages and your past support answers. It replies from those, so it isn’t inventing anything or fumbling questions your team answers daily.

  • Your documents, not guesses
  • Stays current
  • Says when it doesn’t know
02STEP 02

It turns questions into qualified leads

While answering, it works out whether this visitor is worth your team’s time, budget, timing, what they’re after, and books the good ones straight into a calendar.

  • Qualifies as it talks
  • Books meetings
  • Filters out tyre-kickers
03STEP 03

Your team sees everything

Every conversation lands in one place with the outcome attached. You can see what people keep asking, which is usually the fastest way to find what your website isn’t explaining.

  • Full transcripts
  • Live handover
  • Spot the gaps in your site

    Common questions

    The things people ask first.

    How is this different from the chatbots that never work?

    Older chatbots follow a decision tree and break the moment someone asks something off-script. This reads your documents and answers in its own words, then says so plainly when it doesn’t know.

    Can it make things up?

    It answers from your source material and is set to defer rather than guess. Everything is logged so you can check what it’s saying.

    How long does setup take?

    Usually one to two weeks, most of which is gathering the documents it learns from.

    What if someone wants a human?

    They ask, and it hands over. Outside hours it takes details and books a callback.

    What happens to the conversations visitors have with it?

    They land in your systems and stay yours. Every conversation is stored with the full transcript and the outcome attached, so you can read back exactly what was asked and what was answered. Nothing sits locked in a dashboard you cannot export, and we agree the retention period with you before it goes live.
