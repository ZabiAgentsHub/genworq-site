SOLUTIONS / MARKETING AUTOMATION

Campaigns that run without you.

Written, timed, sent and followed up, without the weekly scramble. Your list segments itself as people behave differently, the right message reaches the right person, and the ones who showed interest get chased before they forget you exist.

Marketing automation is software that runs campaigns on your behalf, writing and sending messages, segmenting your audience by behaviour, and triggering follow-up based on what people do rather than on someone remembering.

How it works

How does marketing automation with Genworq work? Three steps.

01STEP 01

Campaigns get written and scheduled

Agents draft in your voice, build the sequence and schedule it. You review before anything sends, the difference is that the work is done and waiting rather than sitting on a list.

  • Drafts in your voice
  • You approve first
  • Ready, not pending
02STEP 02

Your list sorts itself out

People move between segments based on what they actually do, what they opened, what they ignored, what they bought. No more exporting a spreadsheet to work out who to send what.

  • Behaviour-based segments
  • Always current
  • No manual lists
03STEP 03

Interest gets followed up while it’s warm

Someone clicks pricing three times, or opens every email and never replies. That triggers the next message straight away, instead of waiting for the next scheduled send.

  • Triggered by behaviour
  • Chases warm interest
  • Hands over to sales

    Common questions

    The things people ask first.

    Do we lose control of what goes out?

    No. You approve campaigns before they send. The automation handles preparation, timing and follow-up, not the decision to publish.

    Does AI-written email still work?

    It works when it’s specific and in your voice, and fails when it’s generic. Everything is drafted from your material and reviewed by you before sending.

    Which platforms do you work with?

    We build on top of your existing stack rather than moving you onto something new.

    How do we know it’s working?

    You get the numbers that matter, replies, meetings booked, revenue attributed, not just open rates.

    Will sending more automatically hurt our deliverability?

    No, provided what goes out stays relevant, which is what the behaviour-based segments are for. Volume is not what puts mail in the spam folder; sending the same thing to people who never open it is. Because segments update on what people actually do, the disengaged quietly stop receiving campaigns instead of being mailed harder, so your sender reputation holds.
