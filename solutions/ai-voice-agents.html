    Common questions

    The things people ask first.

    What is an AI voice agent?

    An AI voice agent is software that answers and holds a real-time phone conversation, understands the caller’s intent, and takes action, like booking a meeting or logging a support ticket, without a human on the line.

    How is this different from a regular phone tree (IVR)?

    A phone tree makes callers navigate fixed menu options. An AI voice agent has an actual conversation, it understands open-ended questions, asks follow-ups, and adapts, rather than routing on keypresses.

    Does it sound robotic?

    It handles interruptions, callers can cut across it mid-sentence and it adapts, which is the part scripted phone systems cannot do. The speech is natural rather than synthetic, and replies come back in real time, so it holds a conversation instead of reading from a script.

    What happens if the AI can’t handle the call?

    It hands off to a person with the full transcript and context already attached, so your team isn’t starting the conversation over.

    How long does setup take?

    Most teams have a working AI voice agent handling real calls within one to two weeks, depending on how many call types and integrations are in scope.
