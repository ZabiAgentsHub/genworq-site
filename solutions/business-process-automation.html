    Common questions

    The things people ask first.

    How long before something is actually running?

    Most first processes go live in two to four weeks. We start with one process rather than your whole operation, so you see it working before you commit further.

    What happens when the automation gets something wrong?

    Every action is logged and reversible. Anything the system isn’t confident about gets held for a human instead of guessed at. You decide which steps always need sign-off.

    Do we have to replace the software we already use?

    No. We build on top of your existing accounts package, CRM, inbox and spreadsheets. Nothing gets thrown out and nobody learns a new system.

    How do you decide which process to automate first?

    We look for high volume, low judgement and clear rules. The job that eats the most hours and involves the least thinking is almost always the right place to start.

    What does it cost?

    Price is driven by two things: how many processes are in scope, and how many systems each one touches. A single process running across two or three systems sits at the low end; several processes spanning your accounts package, CRM and inbox sits higher. We scope it first, so you get a fixed price and an estimate of hours saved before any work starts.
