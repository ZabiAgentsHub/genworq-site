Solutions

AI agents for every part of the business that’s stuck on busywork.

Five purpose-built AI systems, each solving one part of the operation: marketing, sales, support, or back office. Start with the one costing you the most hours, or run several together.

GENWORQ’s solutions are five agentic AI systems that plug into the tools you already use and run the work end to end. Not assist with it, run it. With people gating the decisions that matter.

Business Process Automation

Order handling, invoicing, procurement, onboarding and support, run end to end inside the systems you already use. Fewer manual touches, fewer errors, and work that finishes overnight instead of next week.

AI Voice Agents

AI Chatbots
